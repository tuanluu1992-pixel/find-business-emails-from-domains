# Find IT Director Email Addresses from Company Websites

IT directors oversee critical technology infrastructure decisions, making them valuable contacts for enterprise software vendors, cybersecurity firms, and infrastructure providers. Rather than manually searching LinkedIn or company websites, you can automate the discovery of verified IT director emails.

The [Website Contact Enrichment Actor](https://apify.com/qualifyops/website-contact-enrichment) crawls target company websites, identifies IT leadership from organizational structures and team pages, and extracts verified email addresses linked to domain names. The actor validates each email against actual domain records to ensure deliverability before you use them for outreach.

This approach scales efficiently—you can process hundreds of company websites simultaneously to build a qualified list of IT decision-makers. The actor handles variations in title naming (IT Director, Chief Information Officer, VP of Technology) and organizations with distributed teams.

Use this data for strategic B2B research, market analysis, or identifying companies evaluating technology infrastructure upgrades.
